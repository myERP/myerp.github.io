Reconciliations

{% highlight bash %}Endpoint: {{ site.api.base_url }}{{ site.api.v1}}{{ site.resource.reconciliations.url}}{% endhighlight %}

Reconciliations link a Bank Entry to an accounting document, confirming that the bank transaction and the document represent the same financial event.

You must provide a bank_entry id and a budget_category id when creating a reconciliation. Providing a document_type is obligatory for matching an existing document, optional otherwise: if omitted, the type is inferred automatically from the bank entry amount and budget category type (see Auto-detection below).
An invoice, vendor bill, credit memo or vendor credit memo can be matched as a whole or through one of its installments (see Installments below).
Deleting a reconciliation unreconciles the document from the bank entry and updates the bank entry status accordingly.

Auto-detection of Document Type

When document_type is omitted from a POST request, the type is inferred automatically from the bank entry amount and budget category type:

Bank Entry AmountBudget Category TypeInferred Type
Negative (debit)EXPENSE, INCOME, or UNCATEGORIZEDvendor_bill — creates a Vendor Bill
Positive (credit)EXPENSE, INCOME, or UNCATEGORIZEDsales_receipt — creates a Sales Receipt
Any non-zeroTRANSFERbank_transfer — creates a Bank Transfer

To reconcile with any other document type, document_type must be provided explicitly.

Matching an Existing Document

When document.id references an existing document, the bank entry is matched with an accounting entry of that document. These rules apply to the document types bank_payment, sales_receipt, bank_transfer, customer_payment, vendor_payment, vendor_bill, customer_invoice, credit_memo, vendor_credit_memo, bank_deposit and the *_installment types. They do not apply to accounting_receipt, accounting_entry, fixed_asset and cash_drawer_closing.

  • An Invoice, Vendor Bill, Credit Memo or Vendor Credit Memo must be finalized, otherwise it has no accounting yet and the request is refused (error 3030). A document created from a bank entry (source_type bank_entry) is matched even when it is not finalized.
  • A document can only be matched with a bank entry in its own currency. This applies to invoices, vendor bills, credit memos and their installments, and to existing expenses and sales receipts.
  • The bank entry is matched with the first payment of the document that fits in what is left to reconcile on the bank entry. A payment already deposited only counts when it is on the bank entry's bank account. If none fits and the document is not fully paid, it is matched through its unpaid installment with the first due date. A bank entry smaller than that installment pays it partially.
  • Any other accounting entry must fit in what is left to reconcile on the bank entry. When the document has several candidate entries, the oldest one is taken first and only then checked: if it is larger than what is left on the bank entry, nothing is matched, even when a newer entry would fit.
  • If no accounting entry of the document can be matched with the bank entry (already matched, already paid, paid on another bank account, larger than the bank entry, or in another currency), the request is refused (error 3032) and nothing is saved.
  • An existing payment keeps its status: only a payment generated by the match is set as draft when the bank reconciliation review step is enabled.
  • An existing Vendor Bill keeps its lines: budget_category and tax only change the main account line of a vendor bill created from a bank entry (source_type bank_entry). Changing its tax keeps its total, paid by the bank entry: only its price without taxes is computed again.

bank_transfer

Reconciles a bank entry with a Bank Transfer (user_code: BTRF-xxx). The transfer can be an existing one or created inline via the document field.
Bank entry direction: positive or negative depending on which side of the transfer is being reconciled.
The payee field is automatically set to the counterpart bank account of the transfer.

Incomplete transfer: an existing transfer missing one of its bank accounts is matched through the leg on the bank entry's own side, the credited leg for a credit and the debited leg for a debit. When that leg has no bank account, the match fills it in with the bank entry's own bank account; the payee stays the counterpart bank account. The incomplete leg is in the accounting currency, so only a bank entry on a bank account in that currency can complete it. Deleting the reconciliation then only resets that bank account: the transfer is kept (delete_on_unmatch is false), and returning the match to review only moves the matched leg back to draft.
A transfer generated by the match is still deleted with it, even when its bank account is filled in afterwards.

documentFull Bank Transfer object (inline creation) or {id, user_code} to reference an existing one.
payeeThe counterpart bank account. Contains id, name, and type (BANK_ACCOUNT).

Installments

A bank entry can be matched with a single installment of a finalized document (or one created from a bank entry), instead of the document as a whole. Use one of the *_installment document types and give the id of the installment as document.id (or document_id).
The installment is matched like its document, with the same budget category, through the forecast accounting entry of the installment. A bank entry smaller than the installment pays it partially.
Like any match through a payment of the document, the reconciliation is saved on the payment it generates: it is then returned with the type of the document (e.g. customer_invoice for an invoice_installment).

document_typedocument.idBank entry directionReturned document_type
invoice_installmentid of an Installment of an Invoicepositive (credit)customer_invoice
vendor_bill_installmentid of an installment of a Vendor Billnegative (debit)vendor_bill
credit_memo_installmentid of an installment of a Credit Memonegative (debit)credit_memo
vendor_credit_memo_installmentid of an installment of a Vendor Credit Memopositive (credit)vendor_credit_memo

Errors:

  • An unknown installment, or the installment of another kind of document, is refused as an out of range document.id (or document_id).
  • An installment already paid has no accounting entry anymore: the request is refused (error 3031).
  • An installment of a document that is not finalized is refused (error 3030), unless the document was created from a bank entry.
  • A bank entry in another currency than the document is refused (error 3032).
